## Materials

- Raspberry Pi 5
- [Pimoroni NVMe Base](https://shop.pimoroni.com/products/nvme-base)

:::info Only if you cut the case yourself
- 4 mm plywood
- Plexiglass
- 4 wood screws, 3 × 8 mm
- 40 × 40 mm fan and fan grill
- M2.5 washers
- Laser cutter with more than 1000 W
:::

## Tools

- Wood glue
- Small flat-head and T10 screwdrivers

## Steps

1. Set up the Raspberry Pi and NVMe Base, leaving out 2 of the 4 screws diagonally.
2. Glue the box together. Make sure all parts face the right way.
3. Screw the fan into its position.
4. Insert the Raspberry Pi kit and fasten it from below with the 2 remaining stand-off screws.
5. Connect the fan pins.
6. Attach the plexiglass panel with the wood screws.

:::note
The rubber feet of the NVMe Base can stay on during assembly.
:::
